Terms and conditions

These terms explain how BiteRush orders, delivery, payment, and customer information are handled.

Orders and payment

Orders, feedback, menu settings, and delivery details are saved in this browser and are not sent to a restaurant or shared across devices. Cash and online payment selections do not process a payment.

Delivery

Delivery times and delivery options are estimates. Priority, Saver, Standard, and scheduled options may change the delivery charge shown in your order summary.

Changes and cancellations

Please review your order carefully before selecting Order now. Contact BiteRush as soon as possible if an order needs correction.

Privacy

Information entered during checkout remains in local browser storage. Do not enter real payment credentials or sensitive personal information. Clearing this browser’s site data can remove saved orders and settings.

Contact and support

No customer-support channel is currently available. Before accepting real orders, add a verified contact method and connect checkout to a secure backend.
